Bylaws of the Riverside Food Co-op

Article 1. Purpose

1.1 The Co-op runs a members' grocery shop at 3 Mill Lane and sells food at fair prices.

Article 2. Members

2.1 Each member has one vote. 2.2 Members vote on spending from the treasury.

Article 3. Spending

3.1 The treasury may pay for food stock, rent, repairs, equipment for the shop and the costs of running it.

3.2 No single payment may exceed 0.002 GEN.

3.3 No payment may go to the member who proposed it or to a business that member owns.

3.4 The treasury may not pay for gifts, parties or political campaigns.

Article 4. Changes

4.1 These bylaws change only by a new organisation founded with the new text.
